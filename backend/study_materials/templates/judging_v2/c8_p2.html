The First China Smart Retail Conference will be Held on March 18th
In the era of "Internet+", smart retail has become a major factor in driving brand influence. The "First China Smart Retail Conference", hosted by the Smart Retail Committee of the China Digital Commerce Association under the auspices of the Chinese Ministry of Trade, will be held at the Beijing National Convention Center from March 18 to 22, 2018, to assist Chinese brands in building digital transformation and upgrading, while promoting the development of the entire smart retail industry chain ecosystem.
The theme of this conference is "Building the Right Path in the Intelligent Digital Era", which will bring comprehensive discussions on smart retail in the era of Internet+ from a global perspective. The conference has attracted internet giants such as Haitu, Qiantang, Beiyuan, Northsky, Kanyu, Qingzhou, Weiyu, Datamere, Adverra, and hundreds of domestic and foreign brand owners and innovative smart retail companies to participate.
Facing the new challenges encountered by Chinese brand building in the "Internet+" era, combined with the achievements of the smart retail industry, the conference has set up eight major awards, aiming to encourage and recognize brand owners, digital companies, media institutions, and practitioners who innovate through digitization.
The inaugural China Smart Retail Conference will bring about a new integration of the entire Internet ecosystem, becoming the weather vane of smart retail in 2018.
