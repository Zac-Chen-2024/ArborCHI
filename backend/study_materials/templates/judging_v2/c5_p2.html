To Build a New Vision for Games & Empower the New Future of Esports Optics
Guangzhou Sep. 15, 2023 / Newswire Asia / -- The Award Ceremony of 9th Blue Lantern Award was held in Beijing on Sep. 12, 2023. Selected project of LUMEN Optics - LUMEN \times RX Esports Club: To Empower the New Future of Esports Optics stood out from over 3,200 case works submitted by brand owners, agencies, media advertising, technology companies, and other altogether more than 640 participating companies to win the Silver Award in the Marketing Case Category.
