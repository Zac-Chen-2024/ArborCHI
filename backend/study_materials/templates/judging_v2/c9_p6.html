Smart Retail Committee of China will promote the communication and integration between the smart retail industry and brand enterprises through industry seminars, subject research, trend release, industry standard formulation and other methods, and will hold the "First China Smart Retail Conference", at the same time, will also release the Anti- Internet Advertising Traffic Hijacking Convention, China Mobile Marketing Standards and other industry standards, as well the annual China Smart Retail Industry Report, China Brand Digital Assets List and other major industry trend research reports. In addition, the Committee will assist the industry to use all resources and networks to promote the development of the new smart retail economy, attract more talented people, and promote brand owners to understand the value of technological innovation and smart retail.
