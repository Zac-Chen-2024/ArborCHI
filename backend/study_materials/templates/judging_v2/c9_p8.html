About Smart Retail Committee of China
Smart Retail Committee of China is composed of 19 founding member entities: Datamere, Avazu, Adwo Media, Beiyuan, Hexing Yuanlu, Adverra, Northsky, Hylink, Lanxin Digital Network, BlueFocus, Yunqi, Ruikan Technology, AdMaster Technology, SunTeng Technology, Haitu, ADTIME, Kanyu, Vooie, Trendscope & Qingnuo Consulting. And the Smart Retail Committee of China has set up five working groups, respectively in international cooperation, data technology, mobile marketing, digital media, regional development and other fields, to carry out a series of work conducive to the development of smart retail industry.
