As the ever first national association of advertising industry, China Digital Commerce Association was founded in 1984 and successively named as China Digital Commerce Association of Foreign Trade, abbreviated as CDCFET and China Digital Commerce Association of Trade, abbreviated as CDCFT. In September 2007, with the restructuring of the governmental organizations and approved by the Ministry of Trade and the Ministry of Public Affairs, China Digital Commerce Association of Trade officially changed its name into China Digital Commerce Association, CDCA in short.
