After the appointment ceremony, Mr. Meilin Guo, Chairman of the jury, shared breakthroughs of the 9th Blue Lantern Award in terms of competition collection data and the composition of judges of this session's Blue Lantern Award. Taking the post of Chairman of the jury of Blue Lantern Award for the second time, Meilin Guo expressed his gratitude to the trust of the organizing committee of Blue Lantern Award, "It's not only an honor, but also a trust".
Later, Meilin Guo made a detailed instruction on the collection of the awards. He mentioned that the 9th Blue Lantern Award altogether received more than 3,200 case works of various categories (with a year- on- year growth of 22\% ) from more than 640 participating companies (with a year- on- year growth of 17\% ). Over 50\% 's participating companies were selected out for final review upon online review, to be specific, 416 companies. As to the whole case works, more than 1,000 works were selected out for final review, with the proportion of finalists of case works not higher than 27.5\% of the total cases.
In the end, Meilin Guo reminded and specified to the members of the presidium again to exercise functions and powers of reviewer with caution, and to lead the panel judges with impartiality, objectivity, high standards and strict requirements, to select out excellent works that could represent the positive values of the industry.
