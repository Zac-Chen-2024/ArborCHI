Final Review of the 9th Blue Lantern Award Ended Successfully! _ Review _ Case _ Process
[ photograph ]
At the beginning of the final review, Mr. Zhiyuan Tan, Founder of Blue Lantern Award, sincerely expressed his gratitude to the judges in the key words of "feelings" and "rigorous", and further emphasized on the process and rules of the final review.
Facing the sudden disruption of rhythm, he mentioned that "Although failing to meet you in person, sometimes regret shows how valuable it is to meet later", encouraged every attending judge with a positive attitude, and noted that the quality of the final review of Blue Lantern Award will not be affected by the change of the review mode.
In addition, facing the large increase in the quantity of various kinds of cases, Zhiyuan Tan said it's both motivation and pressure. Considering the actual time factor, the term for this final review was set as three days. Compared with the previous offline final reviews, the term for judges' scoring was prolonged, so as to finish review in high quality.
Release of Review Members by Meilin Guo, Chairman of the Jury
