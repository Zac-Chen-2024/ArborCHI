About Blue Lantern Award
Blue Lantern Award is the comprehensive award of the most professional influence in the digitization of Chinese brand marketing. Guided by the Smart Retail Committee of China Digital Commerce Association, and adhering to the purpose of "being building personnel of the industry", Blue Lantern Award is constantly outputting various kinds of value contents for the industry and brand enterprises.
Blue Lantern Award has a large jury made up of more than 600 well- known practical experts in the industry. Upon online review, offline final review, defense for gold award and other rigorous and normative review process, it always maintains a gold award winning rate of 1\% . Its principle of putting quality before quantity and ensuring value has selected out and recorded excellent cases of each year for the industry, and has outputted valuable contents to the industry through case insight.
