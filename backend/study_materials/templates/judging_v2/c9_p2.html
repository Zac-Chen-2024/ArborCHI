Smart Retail Committee of China Digital Commerce Association Was Established in Beijing
Jul.30,2018 Source:Unknown
On Jun. 25, 2017, the Smart Retail Committee of China Digital Commerce Association (hereinafter referred as "Smart Retail Committee of China") was established in Beijing.
Smart Retail Committee of China (SRCC) is a non- profit organization that is voluntarily initiated and founded by outstanding Internet technology enterprises, smart retail service enterprises, data technology research and service institutions, digital media and other entities and industry experts in China, focusing on the field of smart retail. The member entities include the world famous technology enterprises, large platform group companies, industrial Internet ecological companies, big data technology companies, mobile Internet companies, smart retail comprehensive companies, large Internet of Things application enterprises, digital media groups, digital business research institutions, etc.
