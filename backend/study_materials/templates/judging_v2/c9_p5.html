(Chenyu Bai, Executive Vice Chairman and Secretary General of China Digital Commerce Association)
The first Chairman and Vice Chairman entities were elected by vote, and Kanyu was elected as the Chairman Entity, with Wei Deng, CEO of Kanyu Media Group, as the Chairman. The Chairman said that the sponsors of the Smart Retail Committee are all pioneers and leaders in the digital field. The focus of the Committee's work is to first bring together new ideas and new driving forces to build a new development platform for "Internet +" in relevant fields and enterprises. At the same time, Beiyuan, Northsky, Haitu Guangdong and Trendscope were elected as Vice Chairman Entities. Upon the nomination by the representative of the Chairman Entity and the approval of resolution of the Council, Lianshu An from Committee Preparatory Office were appointed as the Secretary General, and Mingxu Cao was appointed as the Deputy Secretary General. At the same time, five professional working groups including International Cooperation Group, Data Technology Group, Mobile Marketing Group, Digital Media Group and Regional Development Group were set up to carry out the annual work and Northsky, Haitu Guangdong, Beiyuan, Kanyu and Trendscope will serve as the group leaders, and other
