As the Chairman of the supervision and examination committee, Mr. Hao Qin interpreted the process and rules of final review for the concerned judges.
Firstly, Hao Qin explained the major adjustment to the structural setting of the jury of this year's Blue Lantern Award, where it's the first time to place the supervision and examination committee independent from the jury, and the supervision and examination committee would take full charge of the supervision and examination process and the quality control, and be accountable to the organizing committee of Blue Lantern Award together with the jury.
Later, he emphasized judge avoidance, motions and seconds for panel discussion and other important principles involved in the final review of this Blue Lantern Award, and read out the Confidentiality Undertaking of Judge of the 9th (2023- 2023) Blue Lantern Award, to urge the supervision and examination chairmen to exercise respective functions and powers independently without interference, and to insist on review principles and bottom lines.
Speech of Yanshu Wei, Chairman of China Digital Commerce Association & Chairman of Organizing Committee of Blue Lantern Award
