After entering the 21st century, Professor Xu continuously published the "Social Fracture Trilogy" ("Fracture," "Imbalance," "Game"), which proposed the characteristics of the transition process in China's social structure and received widespread attention from the academic community and the public.
Before joining Jinling University in 2000, from 1982 to 2000, Professor JianwenXu was at the Department of Sociology, Nanhu University, successively serving as an assistant, lecturer, and associate professor.
Professor Jianwen Xu's main research areas include social development, changes in social structure, and oral social history, among others.
