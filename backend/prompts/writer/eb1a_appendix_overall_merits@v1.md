OVERALL MERITS — FINAL MERITS DETERMINATION (Kazarian Step 2):

LEGAL FRAMEWORK:
Under Kazarian v. USCIS, 596 F.3d 1115 (9th Cir. 2010), after the Beneficiary
has satisfied the initial evidentiary threshold (at least three of the ten criteria
under 8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)), the adjudicator must conduct a final merits
determination to evaluate whether the TOTALITY of the evidence demonstrates
sustained national or international acclaim and that the Beneficiary is among
the small percentage at the very top of the field.

STRUCTURE (mandatory):
1. TOTALITY DECLARATION — Open with a statement summarizing ALL criteria already
   established in earlier sections. Reference each criterion by name and its key
   achievement (1-2 sentences per criterion).
2. SUPPLEMENTAL EVIDENCE BY THEME — For each piece of supplemental evidence moved
   into this section, argue how it REINFORCES the overall picture of acclaim.
   Cross-reference criteria where the same evidence supports multiple standards.
3. SYNTHESIS — Draw connections across criteria: how awards corroborate leadership,
   how publications validate original contributions, how judging roles confirm
   peer recognition, etc.
4. TOTALITY CONCLUSION — Conclude that, taken together, the evidence unmistakably
   places the Beneficiary among the small percentage at the very top of the field
   with sustained national/international acclaim.

CROSS-REFERENCE RULES:
- When referencing criteria established in earlier sections, if the SAME exhibit
  appears in your SNIPPET INDEX, cite it normally with [Exhibit X, p.Y].
- If the exhibit is NOT in your SNIPPET INDEX, restate the fact WITHOUT any
  exhibit citation — just write the sentence naturally.
- NEVER fabricate citation formats like [Cross-reference Section X] or [See above].
