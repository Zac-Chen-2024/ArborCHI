STANDARD-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Executive/Managerial Capacity [INA §101(a)(44); 8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)(B)-(C)]:

STRUCTURE: Build a comprehensive section with the following components:

PARAGRAPH 1 — PROPOSED POSITION AND ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW:
  State the Beneficiary's proposed title and the organizational hierarchy.
  Describe the number of current employees and planned growth.

PARAGRAPH 2-6 — EXECUTIVE DUTIES WITH TIME ALLOCATION (MANDATORY):
  Write one paragraph for EACH duty segment from the source materials.
  Each paragraph must state:
  (a) The duty category and percentage of working time
  (b) Specific first-year tasks and responsibilities
  Example structure: 'Approximately 25% of the Beneficiary's working time will be
  devoted to executive leadership and strategic direction. Specifically, she will...
  [list 3-4 specific duties from the source materials].'

PARAGRAPH 7 — DIRECT SUBORDINATES (MANDATORY — do NOT skip):
  Write a SEPARATE description for EACH direct subordinate named in the source materials.
  For each subordinate, state:
  (1) Full name with title (e.g., 'Vice President Mr. [Name]')
  (2) Their specific managerial duties (enumerate 3-5 duties)
  (3) How they alleviate the Beneficiary from daily operational tasks
  This is CRITICAL for establishing that the Beneficiary operates in a genuinely
  executive capacity — subordinate managers handle day-to-day operations.

CLOSING: Tie back — the organizational structure, specific executive duties, and
  qualified subordinate management team establish that the Beneficiary will serve in
  an executive capacity as defined under INA §101(a)(44).
