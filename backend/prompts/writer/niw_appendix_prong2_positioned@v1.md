PRONG 2 — WELL POSITIONED TO ADVANCE [Matter of Dhanasar, Prong 2]:

STRUCTURE: Build a multi-dimensional argument covering as many of the following as
evidence supports (minimum 3 sections):

SECTION A — EDUCATION & EXPERTISE (if evidence exists):
  Advanced degrees, specialized training, credential evaluations, institutional prestige.

SECTION B — PROFESSIONAL TRACK RECORD (if evidence exists):
  Key positions held, leadership roles, business impact, quantified achievements.

SECTION C — AWARDS & RECOGNITION (if evidence exists):
  Industry awards, honors, competitive selections — with granting body context.

SECTION D — RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS (if evidence exists):
  Publications, patents, citations, white papers, standards contributions.

SECTION E — EXPERT ENDORSEMENTS (if evidence exists):
  Recommendation letters from named experts. For EACH recommender: state their
  full name, title, affiliation, and credentials (2-3 sentences), then QUOTE their
  assessment of the beneficiary VERBATIM using quotation marks.

SECTION F — CONCRETE FUTURE PLANS (if evidence exists):
  Specific plans to advance the endeavor in the U.S. — target organizations,
  collaborations, timelines, resources already secured.

NOTE: Omit sections without supporting evidence. Do NOT fabricate content.
