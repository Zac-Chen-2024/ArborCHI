PRONG 3 — WAIVER JUSTIFICATION [Matter of Dhanasar, Prong 3]:

STRUCTURE: Build a comprehensive waiver argument with ALL of the following components:

COMPONENT A — IMPRACTICALITY OF LABOR CERTIFICATION (MANDATORY):
  Explain why the PERM labor certification process is unsuitable for this
  beneficiary's proposed endeavor. Focus on: (1) the nature of the work requires
  flexibility, multi-institutional collaboration, or self-direction that an
  employer-specific PERM would constrain; (2) the PERM process's employer-tied
  structure conflicts with the cross-cutting nature of the work; (3) specific
  time delays from PERM would meaningfully hinder progress on the endeavor.
  Use facts from the SOURCE MATERIALS about the beneficiary's planned work.

COMPONENT B — NATIONAL BENEFIT ANALYSIS (MANDATORY):
  Articulate concrete national benefits from the beneficiary's specific
  contributions — do NOT make generic field-level claims. Connect to specific
  government priorities, strategic initiatives, or policy goals mentioned in
  the source materials. Where available, cite quantitative impact: jobs created,
  revenue generated, efficiency improvements, or populations served. Argue that
  these benefits exist even if other qualified U.S. workers are available.

COMPONENT C — BEYOND SINGLE EMPLOYER (MANDATORY):
  Demonstrate that the beneficiary's work transcends any single employer's
  interests. Evidence: multi-institutional collaborations, standards-setting,
  open research/publications, industry-wide impact, consulting across orgs.
  If CROSS-PRONG CONTEXT shows such activities, reference them here WITHOUT
  fabricating exhibit citations.

COMPONENT D — URGENCY / TIME-SENSITIVITY (IF APPLICABLE):
  If source materials mention specific timelines, appointments, grant deadlines,
  or competitive landscapes, argue that delays from labor certification would
  create meaningful disadvantage. Omit if no time-sensitive evidence exists.

COMPONENT E — EXPLICIT BALANCING (MANDATORY — must be FINAL component):
  Explicitly weigh national interest against labor market protection. Use legal
  balancing language: 'on balance', 'the national interest in [X] outweighs the
  purpose of the labor certification requirement'. Connect back to Prong 1 and 2
  accomplishments. Conclude with clear waiver justification.

CROSS-PRONG REFRAMING TECHNIQUE:
  Prong 3 is a POLICY argument, not merely an evidence-listing exercise. REFRAME
  accomplishments from Prongs 1 and 2 through a waiver justification lens. Do not
  simply restate them — explain WHY each accomplishment makes labor certification
  impractical or contrary to the national interest.
