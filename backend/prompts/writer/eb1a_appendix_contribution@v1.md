CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Original Contribution [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(v)]:

CRITICAL — PERSON NAMING RULES (apply throughout this section):
  - Every time you reference a person from the source materials, use their FULL NAME
    with a professional title prefix: Dr., Prof., Mr., Ms., Director, Chairman, etc.
    NEVER write 'an expert' or 'a trainee' when the source materials provide a name.
  - For recommendation letter writers and endorsers, always state: full name, title,
    affiliation, and then QUOTE their specific assessment in quotation marks.
    Example: 'Dr. Bangchang Xie, Vice President of Fu Jen Catholic University,
    stated in his recommendation letter: "Professor Liu has actively contributed
    to the establishment of academic communities..."'

STRUCTURE: Write one section per contribution. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — TECHNICAL DESCRIPTION OF THE CONTRIBUTION:
  Describe the innovation, methodology, system, or technique the Beneficiary
  developed. Explain what problem it solves and how it differs from prior
  approaches. Use specific technical details from the source materials —
  algorithms, architectures, formulations, or design principles.

PARAGRAPH 2 — ADOPTION AND COMMERCIALIZATION:
  Provide concrete evidence of real-world adoption. Include quantitative data:
  page views, downloads, orders, revenue, user counts, or deployment scale.
  If the contribution was commercialized into a product or service, describe
  the market impact with specific numbers from the source materials.

PARAGRAPH 3 — NAMED BENEFICIARIES AND DOWNSTREAM OUTCOMES (MANDATORY):
  Write a SEPARATE mini-paragraph for EACH individual who benefited from the
  contribution, as found in the source materials. For each person:
  (1) Full name with title (Mr./Ms./Dr.),
  (2) Their background (nationality, role, affiliation),
  (3) The specific outcome they achieved (e.g., 'was selected as a national
  weightlifting athlete', 'published a cover paper in Nature').
  If recommendation letters describe individual beneficiaries, QUOTE the
  relevant testimony in quotation marks.

PARAGRAPH 4 — EXPERT ENDORSEMENT AND INDEPENDENT VALIDATION (MANDATORY):
  Write a SEPARATE mini-paragraph for EACH expert who endorses the contribution.
  For each expert: state full name, title, affiliation, then QUOTE their specific
  assessment VERBATIM in quotation marks from the source materials. Do NOT
  summarize — use the expert's own words wherever possible.

PARAGRAPH 5 — FIELD-WIDE SIGNIFICANCE:
  Explain how the contribution has changed or advanced the field as a whole.
  Connect to citations, follow-on research, industry standards, or policy
  changes. Argue that the contribution is of MAJOR SIGNIFICANCE, not merely
  incremental improvement.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — demonstrate original
  scientific, scholarly, artistic, athletic, or business-related
  contributions of major significance to the field.
