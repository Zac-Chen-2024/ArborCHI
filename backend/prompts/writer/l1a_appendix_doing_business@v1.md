STANDARD-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Active Business Operations [8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)(H)]:

STRUCTURE: Build a comprehensive section covering BOTH the U.S. and foreign entities:

PARAGRAPH 1 — U.S. ENTITY BUSINESS DESCRIPTION:
  Describe the U.S. entity's core business — products, services, target market.
  Include specific product categories or service lines from the source materials.

PARAGRAPH 2 — U.S. ENTITY FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE:
  State specific revenue figures with dates from the source materials.
  Cite tax returns, bank statements, or audit reports. Include projected
  revenue from the business plan if available.

PARAGRAPH 3 — U.S. ENTITY GROWTH AND HIRING:
  State current employee count and hiring plans. Describe planned departments and
  positions. Provide the timeline for expansion (e.g., '19 employees across five
  divisions within five years').

PARAGRAPH 4 — CUSTOMER AND PARTNER RELATIONSHIPS:
  Name specific customers, partners, or vendors from the source materials.
  Cite cooperation agreements, contracts, invoices, and transaction documents.

PARAGRAPH 5 — FOREIGN PARENT COMPANY OPERATIONS:
  State the parent company's incorporation date, location, number of employees,
  and revenue (in both local currency and USD). Describe its business scope and
  geographic reach. Cite the audit report and business documents.

CLOSING: Tie back — both entities are engaged in regular, systematic, continuous
  provision of goods and services as required under 8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)(H).
