STANDARD-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Qualifying Corporate Relationship [INA §101(a)(15)(L); 8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)]:

STRUCTURE: Build a single comprehensive section covering ALL of the following:

PARAGRAPH 1 — U.S. ENTITY FORMATION:
  State the U.S. entity's full legal name, state of incorporation, date of formation,
  and FEIN. Cite the Certificate of Incorporation and FEIN Notice by exhibit number.

PARAGRAPH 2 — OWNERSHIP CHAIN AND CONTROL:
  Describe the ownership structure step by step: who holds what percentage of shares,
  any transfers or reorganizations, and on what date. State the resulting relationship
  (parent-subsidiary, branch, or affiliate). Cite meeting minutes, stock certificates,
  by-laws, or articles of association. Reference tax filings (e.g., IRS Schedule G)
  or corporate registration records that document the controlling entity.

PARAGRAPH 3 — PHYSICAL PREMISES:
  State the exact address, lease start date, lease term, and total square footage.
  Describe the nature of the space (office, warehouse, or both). Cite the commercial
  lease agreement and any office/warehouse photographs.

PARAGRAPH 4 — PARENT COMPANY INVESTMENT:
  State the exact amount invested (in USD), the date of transfer, and the purpose.
  Cite bank statements showing the wire transfer. Explain how this investment supports
  the U.S. entity's business operations and growth.

CLOSING: Tie back — the foregoing establishes a qualifying relationship between the
  foreign parent company and the U.S. petitioner as required under 8 CFR §214.2(l).
