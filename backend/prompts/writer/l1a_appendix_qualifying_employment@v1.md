STANDARD-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Qualifying Employment Abroad [8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)(A)]:

STRUCTURE: Build a comprehensive section covering the Beneficiary's qualifications:

PARAGRAPH 1 — EDUCATIONAL BACKGROUND:
  State the Beneficiary's degree(s), major, and how their education relates to
  the executive role. Cite degree certificates.

PARAGRAPH 2 — EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND EXECUTIVE EXPERIENCE:
  State the Beneficiary's tenure at the foreign parent company — exact start date,
  title (highest level executive), and duration. Describe prior executive positions
  at other companies if applicable. Emphasize the continuous one-year requirement.

PARAGRAPH 3-4 — EXECUTIVE DUTIES AT FOREIGN ENTITY:
  Describe the Beneficiary's specific executive duties at the foreign entity with
  time allocation percentages. Mirror the U.S. duties structure to show continuity
  of executive function.

PARAGRAPH 5 — SUBORDINATE MANAGEMENT AT FOREIGN ENTITY:
  Name the departments and department managers supervised by the Beneficiary.
  State each manager's name, title, degree, and years of experience.

PARAGRAPH 6 — SPECIFIC ACHIEVEMENTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS:
  Describe concrete business achievements: contracts executed, partnerships
  established, revenue growth, market expansion. Use specific names, dollar
  amounts, and dates from the source materials.

CLOSING: Tie back — the Beneficiary has been employed in an executive capacity
  at the qualifying foreign entity for well over one continuous year within the
  three years preceding this petition, satisfying 8 CFR §214.2(l)(1)(ii)(A).
