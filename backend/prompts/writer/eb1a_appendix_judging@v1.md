CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Judging [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(iv)]:

CRITICAL — PERSON NAMING RULES (apply throughout this section):
  - Every time you reference a person from the source materials, use their FULL NAME
    with a professional title prefix: Dr., Prof., Mr., Ms., Director, Chairman, etc.
    NEVER write 'a judge' or 'an expert' when the source materials provide a name.
  - When naming fellow judges, committee chairs, or panelists, always include their
    title, institutional affiliation, and role.

STRUCTURE: Write one section per judging role. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — ORGANIZATION AND EVENT BACKGROUND:
  Identify the organization, competition, journal, or body for which the
  Beneficiary served as a judge. State its founding, scope (national/
  international), and reputation in the field. Provide the Beneficiary's
  exact title or role — use specific titles found in source materials
  (e.g., 'Deputy Director of the Judging Panel' rather than generic 'judge').

PARAGRAPH 2 — SELECTION AS JUDGE:
  Explain how the Beneficiary was selected or invited to serve as a judge.
  Was it by appointment, election, invitation from a committee? What
  qualifications or expertise were required? This shows the Beneficiary
  was chosen based on recognized expertise.

PARAGRAPH 3 — SCOPE AND SCALE OF JUDGING:
  Quantify the judging responsibilities: how many submissions, applications,
  manuscripts, or entries did the Beneficiary evaluate? Over what time period?
  State the decision weight if available (e.g., 'the panel's recommendations
  carried 80% weightage in final decisions'). Describe the evaluation criteria
  and methodology used.

PARAGRAPH 4 — CO-JUDGE CREDENTIALS AND IMPACT (MANDATORY — do NOT skip):
  Write a SEPARATE mini-paragraph for EACH fellow judge found in the source
  materials. For each person, write 2-3 sentences:
  (1) Full name with title and current position,
  (2) Their institutional affiliation and credentials,
  (3) What their presence on the panel demonstrates about its prestige.
  Then describe the impact of the judging decisions — what happened to
  winners/selectees? This shows the Beneficiary's judgments shaped outcomes.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — demonstrate participation
  as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field.
