CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — High Salary [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(ix)]:
STRUCTURE: Write one section per compensation component. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — COMPENSATION AMOUNT:
  State the exact compensation amount(s). If the salary is in a foreign
  currency, provide both the original amount and the USD equivalent with
  the exchange rate used. Break down total compensation into base salary,
  bonuses, allowances, and any supplemental income (consulting fees,
  speaking honoraria, royalties) found in the source materials.

PARAGRAPH 2 — THIRD-PARTY BENCHMARK:
  Identify the specific benchmark source used for comparison (e.g.,
  SalaryExpert, Bureau of Labor Statistics, PayScale, industry salary
  survey). State the benchmark source's methodology and credibility.
  Provide the benchmark figure for the same role, location, and
  experience level.

PARAGRAPH 3 — MULTIPLIER COMPUTATION:
  COMPUTE the salary multiplier by dividing the Beneficiary's compensation
  by the benchmark (e.g., 'the Beneficiary's annual compensation of
  $185,000 is approximately 2.7 times the median salary of $68,500 for
  comparable professionals in the region'). If multiple benchmarks are
  available, compute the multiplier against each.

PARAGRAPH 4 — SUPPLEMENTAL INCOME (if applicable):
  If the Beneficiary receives additional income beyond base salary
  (consulting, speaking, awards, royalties), list each source with its
  amount. Argue that total remuneration, not just base salary, reflects
  the Beneficiary's market value and extraordinary standing.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — demonstrate that the
  Beneficiary commands a high salary or other significantly high
  remuneration for services in relation to others in the field.
