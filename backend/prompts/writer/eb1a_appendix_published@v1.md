CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Published Material [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(iii)]:

CRITICAL — PERSON NAMING RULES (apply throughout this section):
  - When naming article authors, editors, or experts quoted in the article, use
    their FULL NAME with title prefix: Dr., Prof., Mr., Ms., etc.
  - When naming editorial board members, fellowship committee members, or other
    persons who validate the publication's prestige, include their full credentials.
    Example: 'Prof. Bao Cunkuan of Fudan University serves on the Steering
    Committee of the Sixth Tone Fellowship program.'

STRUCTURE: Write one section per publication. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — PUBLICATION BACKGROUND AND PRESTIGE:
  State the publication's full name, founding year, publisher/owner, and
  circulation figures or readership data. Identify the type of publication
  (newspaper, professional journal, trade magazine, online platform). If
  circulation data is sourced from an authority (e.g., Library of Congress,
  Ulrich's, press association records), cite that authority BY NAME. Mention
  any press awards the publication itself has received, naming the award.

PARAGRAPH 2 — EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE AND STANDARDS:
  Explain the editorial process — does the publication have an independent
  editorial board? Name board members if found in source materials. Is content
  peer-reviewed, editor-selected, or staff-written? If the publication has
  fellowship programs or oversight committees, name the members with their
  credentials. This establishes that the coverage was not self-promotional.

PARAGRAPH 3 — COVERAGE SCOPE AND CONTENT:
  Describe what the published material covers about the Beneficiary. Prove
  that it is ABOUT the Beneficiary and their work (not merely a passing
  mention). QUOTE specific passages from the article using quotation marks
  to demonstrate substantive coverage of the Beneficiary's achievements.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — establish that this constitutes
  published material about the Beneficiary in professional or major trade
  publications or other major media, relating to the Beneficiary's work in
  the field.
