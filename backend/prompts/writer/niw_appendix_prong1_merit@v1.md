PRONG 1 — SUBSTANTIAL MERIT & NATIONAL IMPORTANCE [Matter of Dhanasar, Prong 1]:

STRUCTURE: Build a two-part argument with BOTH of the following sections:

SECTION A — SUBSTANTIAL MERIT (MANDATORY):
  Define the beneficiary's proposed endeavor precisely. Then demonstrate its
  substantial merit with concrete evidence: technical innovation, societal benefit,
  industry recognition, or solved problems. Use specific metrics, outcomes, and
  expert assessments from the source materials. If recommendation letters discuss
  the endeavor's value, QUOTE the recommender by full name and title.

SECTION B — NATIONAL IMPORTANCE (MANDATORY):
  Prove the endeavor's importance extends beyond the beneficiary's personal interests
  to a national scale. Evidence includes: government policy alignment (executive orders,
  legislation, agency priorities), economic impact data (market size, job creation,
  GDP contribution), broad applicability across sectors or populations, and strategic
  necessity (national security, infrastructure, public health). Cite specific statistics
  and policy documents from the source materials.
