CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Awards [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(i)]:

CRITICAL — PERSON NAMING RULES (apply throughout this section):
  - Every time you reference a person from the source materials, use their FULL NAME
    with a professional title prefix: Dr., Prof., Mr., Ms., Director, Chairman, etc.
    Example: 'Dr. Qiji Ma, a Researcher at the China National Institute of Advertising'
    NEVER write 'a researcher' or 'an expert' when the source materials provide a name.
  - When naming committee members, judges, or reviewers, always include their title,
    institutional affiliation, and role. Example: 'Prof. Shyam Sundar, founder of the
    Media Effects Research Laboratory at Penn State University'

STRUCTURE: Write one section per award. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — GRANTING BODY BACKGROUND:
  Identify the organization that grants the award. State its founding year, governance
  structure, and mission. Explain why this body is authoritative in the field. If the
  organization is government-affiliated, chartered, or internationally recognized,
  state that explicitly with supporting details from the source materials.

PARAGRAPH 2 — SELECTION CRITERIA AND PROCESS:
  Describe the nomination and selection process step by step. If the source materials
  contain charter articles, bylaws, or official rules governing the award, QUOTE the
  relevant text VERBATIM using quotation marks (e.g., 'The Charter states: "candidates
  must demonstrate exceptional contributions..."'). Identify who serves on the
  selection committee by name, title, and credentials. Explain what criteria
  distinguish winners from nominees.

PARAGRAPH 3 — QUANTITATIVE SELECTIVITY:
  Provide the total number of nominees, applicants, or competitors and the number
  of winners. COMPUTE the acceptance rate as a percentage (e.g., '15 out of 648
  nominees were selected, representing an acceptance rate of approximately 2.3%').
  If multi-year data is available, aggregate it. If only partial data is available,
  state what is known and note the implied selectivity.

PARAGRAPH 4 — CO-RECIPIENT CALIBER (MANDATORY — do NOT skip):
  Write a SEPARATE mini-paragraph for EACH co-recipient, past winner, or fellow
  honoree found in the source materials. For each person, write 3-5 sentences:
  (1) Full name with title and current position,
  (2) Their institutional affiliation and role,
  (3) Their key achievements or credentials that show their stature.
  Example: 'Dr. Qiji Ma, a highly regarded figure in market research, holds the
  distinguished position of Researcher at the China National Institute of Advertising.
  As one of the earliest practitioners of market research in mainland China and among
  the first registered consultants in the nation, Dr. Ma also received the "Data
  Legacy of the Year Award."'
  This paragraph demonstrates the Beneficiary is recognized alongside established
  leaders in the field. If no co-recipients are named in source materials, state that
  explicitly rather than omitting the paragraph.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — explain how this award constitutes
  documentation of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for
  excellence in the field of endeavor.
