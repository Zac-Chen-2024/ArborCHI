CRITERION-SPECIFIC ARGUMENTATION — Membership [8 C.F.R. §204.5(h)(3)(ii)]:

CRITICAL — PERSON NAMING RULES (apply throughout this section):
  - Every time you reference a person from the source materials, use their FULL NAME
    with a professional title prefix: Dr., Prof., Mr., Ms., Director, Chairman, etc.
    NEVER write 'a reviewer' or 'an official' when the source materials provide a name.
  - When naming reviewers, board members, or co-members, always include their title,
    institutional affiliation, and role.

STRUCTURE: Write one section per association. Each section must contain ALL of the following paragraphs:

PARAGRAPH 1 — ASSOCIATION BACKGROUND:
  State the association's full name, founding year, headquarters, and mission.
  Describe its role in the field — is it the primary credentialing body, a
  professional society, or an honorary organization? State total membership count
  if available. If it has government recognition or accreditation, state that.

PARAGRAPH 2 — ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS (OUTSTANDING ACHIEVEMENTS):
  QUOTE the association's charter, bylaws, or membership criteria VERBATIM using
  quotation marks. Include article/section numbers when available (e.g., 'The
  Membership Certificate Letter states: "An individual member should have been
  engaged in the industry for more than 10 years or be a senior practitioner..."').
  Walk through the admission process step by step: application submission,
  documentation requirements, review committee evaluation, board vote.

PARAGRAPH 3 — EXPERT JUDGMENT IN SELECTION:
  Identify who evaluates membership applications BY NAME — state each reviewer's
  full name, title, and credentials (e.g., 'Ms. Chong Peng, Vice President of
  the Association and General Manager of Shanghai Donnor Exhibition Service Co.
  Ltd.'). Explain how their expertise qualifies them to judge outstanding
  achievements in the field.

PARAGRAPH 4 — CO-MEMBER CALIBER (MANDATORY — do NOT skip):
  Write a SEPARATE mini-paragraph for EACH notable co-member found in the source
  materials. For each person, write 3-5 sentences:
  (1) Full name with title and current position,
  (2) Their key achievements (awards, championships, positions held),
  (3) How their credentials demonstrate the association's elite membership.
  Example: 'Ms. Ping Zhang, a distinguished member of the Association, won the
  first gold medal in the Asian Bodybuilding and Fitness Championship and received
  the Fitness China Awards "Ten Figures" in 2017. She also served as the coach of
  China's national bodybuilding team.'
  If no co-members are named in source materials, state that explicitly.

CLOSING: Tie back to the regulatory standard — demonstrate that membership
  requires outstanding achievements as judged by recognized national or
  international experts in the field.
